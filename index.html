How it works

A lightweight NLP pipeline running in your browser.

Sentence embeddings
  • Split the response and reference into sentences.
  • Encode sentences with a pretrained MiniLM language model.
  • Compare normalized vectors using cosine similarity.
  • Show the closest reference passage for each response sentence.

Model: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 via Transformers.js.

Interpretation guide

Higher similarity A semantically similar passage was found. It may be relevant, but does not prove the claim is entailed or true.

Moderate similarity The match is partial or ambiguous. Inspect the passage and surrounding context.

Lower similarity No close semantic match was found in the supplied text. This is a review cue, not proof of hallucination.

Research caution
Semantic similarity is not factual verification, textual entailment, or a calibrated confidence score. The model may miss paraphrases, match contradictory text, and overlook important context. Do not use this prototype for medical, legal, financial, or other consequential decisions.

Privacy

Text is processed in your browser after the model downloads. This app does not send your pasted text to an application backend, create accounts, or store analysis history. The model files are fetched from the Hugging Face CDN.
